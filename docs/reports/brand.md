# Brand

If your company has **rebranding** enabled, open **Brand** to customize what your customers see: upload your **logo**, **favicon** and **background image** and set the **brand name**. The branding is applied to the supported products and to the reports.

<Shots items={[{"name": "brand", "caption": "The brand settings."}]} />

If the page says rebranding is not available, ask the owner organization to enable it for your company. Distributors can enable it for their resellers.

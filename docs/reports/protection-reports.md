# Protection reports

**Reports** builds a short report per customer: what the firewalls blocked, the licences that cover the customer and the health of the firewalls. Show it on screen or use **Print or save as PDF** to give it to the customer.

<Shots items={[{"name": "reports", "caption": "A protection report of a customer."}]} />

The report uses the numbers saved by the controller reports. If a customer has none yet, open its controller, go to **Reports** and generate the firewall reports first. When only some firewalls have a recent report, the page says how many are covered and lists the others.

The report is prepared under your brand: see [Brand](brand.md).

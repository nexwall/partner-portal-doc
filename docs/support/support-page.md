# Support

Use **Support** in the menu to ask Nexwall for help without leaving the portal. Everything you and Nexwall write about a problem stays in one ticket, with one history.

## The numbers at the top

- **Open tickets** and the status of each.
- **Closed last month**.
- **Escalated**: tickets that moved to a higher support level (L2, L3 or Development).
- A six-month chart of tickets opened and resolved.

## Open a ticket

1. Click **New ticket**.
2. Pick the firewall (or type its **serial number**). This is optional, but it lets support look at the right firewall.
3. Write a short **subject** and describe **what happens, since when and what you already tried**.
4. Click **Open ticket**. You receive the ticket reference, for example `NX-000123`.

Open one ticket per problem. Never write passwords, keys or licence secrets in a ticket.

## Follow and answer

Click a ticket to see its history. Write your answer and click **Send**. You can **paste a screenshot** (Ctrl+V) or **attach a file**: images, PDF, text or log files and ZIP, up to 10 MB each.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New | The ticket was received. |
| In analysis | Nexwall is working on it. |
| Waiting for you | Nexwall needs your answer or a test from you. |
| L2 support, L3 support, Development | The ticket moved to a higher support level; you keep one history and one reference. |
| Resolved | The problem is solved. You can **Reopen** the ticket within 7 days. |

## Two levels in the same ticket

- **UI and AI guided support**: step-by-step help in the interface.
- **CLI advanced troubleshooting**: diagnostics on the firewall of the ticket. Click **Open advanced troubleshooting** to open the **Support** screen of the customer's Cloud Controller, where you run Support Packets and the results are added to the same ticket. You need permission to manage firewalls for this button.

## E-mail

The person who opened the ticket receives an e-mail with the ticket reference when support answers.

## Good tickets

Include the firewall serial number, what changed before the problem began, the exact error text and a screenshot. The more precise the ticket, the faster the answer.

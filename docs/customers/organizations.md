# Distributors, resellers and customers

The portal has three levels: **distributors** have **resellers**, and resellers have **customers**. You see the levels below your own.

To create one, open **Distributors**, **Resellers** or **Customers** and click **Create**. Fill in the company details; the language you choose becomes the default language of its users and e-mails, and the country follows it.

<Shots items={[{"name": "customers", "caption": "The list of customers with their details."}]} />

- **Edit** changes the company details.
- **Archive** hides the company and its users and firewalls without deleting them.
- **Destroy** removes the company, its users and its firewalls. It cannot be undone.

When a **customer** is created the portal also prepares its Cloud Controller (a few minutes); until then the customer's firewalls show as *being prepared*.

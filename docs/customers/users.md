# Users

Open **Users** to see the people of your company and of the companies below it. Click **Create user** to add one: name, e-mail, company and role.

<Shots items={[{"name": "users", "caption": "The list of users."}]} />

The new user receives a **welcome e-mail** in the language of the company, with a link to set a password. You can edit a user, reset the password, suspend or delete it. Users only see data of their own company and the ones below.

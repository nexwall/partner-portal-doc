# Troubleshooting

**I did not receive the welcome e-mail.** Check the spam folder and that the e-mail address is correct; an administrator can resend it by resetting the password.

**A customer's firewalls are not listed.** The notice says the controller is not answering or is still being prepared. Wait a few minutes and use **Refresh**.

**Open in controller does not work.** Allow pop-ups for the portal. If it still fails, try again in a moment; the controller may be restarting.

**The report has no data.** Open the customer's controller, go to **Reports** and generate the firewall reports first.

**I cannot see a menu item.** Your role or your company may not have it (for example Brand needs rebranding enabled).

Still stuck? Use **Support** in the menu to contact Nexwall.

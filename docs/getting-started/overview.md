# Overview

The Nexwall Partner Portal is where distributors, resellers and their teams manage their Nexwall business.

- **Customers**: create your customers and, if you are a distributor, your resellers.
- **Firewalls, Licences and Alerts**: see every firewall of every customer, their licence status and what needs attention.
- **Reports**: a protection report per customer to show or print.
- **Brand**: your logo, colours and name on the products your customers use.
- **Controller**: each customer has its own Cloud Controller; open it from the portal with one click.
